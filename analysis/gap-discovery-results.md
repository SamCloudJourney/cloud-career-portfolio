# Gap-Discovery Workflow — Final Results

*30 finder agents (UK-only) → 91 candidates → 30 distinct gaps → all 30 adversarially verified for crowding.*
*Run completed 24 June 2026. 61 agents, ~2.8M tokens. Raw data: `gap-discovery-raw.json`.*

## Headline: 3 survived, 27 killed

The adversarial verifiers killed **27 of 30** gaps as already-crowded — including both ideas we'd been most excited about. That ~90% cull rate is the whole point: it stops us building into occupied markets.

## ✅ Survivors (all "MAYBE" — none a clean PURSUE)

### 1. Grid-connection queue & milestone tracker (renewables/BESS) — *best founder-fit*
- **fmf 7 · p(£1m) 4 · verdict: lightly-served**
- Under-served developer-side niche, live 2025-26 milestone/penalty regime, real money at stake.
- **Moat:** continuously-maintained encoding of the GB connections regime (Gate 2, the 8 User Progression Milestones) + workflow lock-in. Domain depth, not tech.
- **$:** £50-150/connection/mo, or per-portfolio seats; white-label to connections consultancies.
- **Catch:** a solo 19-yo with no energy network faces a trust/distribution wall. Best attacked via one consultancy partner as a wedge.
- **Why it's the pick for THIS founder:** maps directly onto his stated obsession (markets/infrastructure/energy/future). Obsessive research is exactly the edge the "no network" weakness needs.

### 2. Alternative Provision (AP) due-diligence for schools/MATs
- **fmf 7 · p(£1m) 4 · verdict: lightly-served**
- Regulation-driven (KCSIE 2025 / "Arranging Alternative Provision" 2025): placing schools must run pre-placement due diligence + minuted half-termly reviews, retaining full safeguarding liability. Done in Word/spreadsheets today; no dedicated SaaS found.
- **Moat:** weak unless you build the two-sided asset — a national, continuously-verified AP-provider directory (provider uploads DBS/insurance/policy once; every commissioning school pulls verified evidence). Without that network effect it's a feature incumbents (The Key, Every, Civica, LearnTrek) bolt on in a quarter.
- **$:** £300-800/yr per school; £2-10k/yr per-MAT central licences.
- **Catch:** slow, low-ACV, term-gated buyers; poor fit for this founder (no interest in schools).

### 3. SEND tribunal appeal pack + working-document assembler for council SEND legal teams
- **fmf 4 · p(£1m) 3 · verdict: lightly-served (maybe-leaning-KILL)**
- Real white space (the A-K tracked-change working-document generator — no product does it). 25k appeals/yr, genuine bar-from-proceedings risk.
- **Catch:** thin and eroding (GenAI commoditises the doc logic; Agilisys/VITA/Liquidlogic circling); councils are glacial buyers; low founder-fit.

## 🧠 The meta-lesson (more valuable than any single gap)

Every *nameable* niche already has 2-3 funded/active startups. This isn't bad luck — it's the defining fact of 2026: **AI made building free for everyone, so white space at the "idea" level is essentially gone.**

Implication: the winning edge has moved away from *finding* an untouched idea (diminishing returns — proven here) toward **execution + distribution + founder obsession** within a space that's *served but not well-served for a specific ignored segment.*

The right question is no longer *"is anyone doing this?"* (always yes) but *"can I serve one ignored slice better than incumbents bother to — and can I actually reach them?"*

**Next step is not more idea-hunting.** It's choosing a space on founder-fit (what you'll obsessively out-grind everyone on) — which points at **grid/energy-infrastructure** — and going deep on the *segment + distribution*, accepting some competition as normal.
